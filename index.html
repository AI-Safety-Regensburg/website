AI Safety Regensburg

A local community for people in Regensburg who want to understand the risks from advanced AI, and help reduce them.

We started in November 2026 and are just getting going. Everyone is welcome, whatever their background, and no prior knowledge of AI is needed.

Join our Signal group Email us

Why AI safety

AI systems are becoming more capable very quickly, while our understanding of how they work, and how to make them reliably do what we intend, lags behind. Many leading researchers, among them Turing Award winners Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio, warn that advanced AI could cause catastrophic harm: through misuse, or through systems pursuing goals nobody intended.

AI safety is the work of preventing this, through technical research, governance and policy, and public understanding. We think it is one of the most important problems of our time, and that people in Regensburg can help with it.

What we're planning

We'd like to start small and grow with the people who join. Our first ideas:

A reading group
Reading key papers and introductory course material, and discussing them together.
Talks and watch parties
Talks by researchers, and screenings of online talks from Safe AI Germany, followed by discussion.
Informal meetups
Getting to know others in Regensburg who care about making advanced AI go well.

Have an idea, a topic, or a venue to suggest? We'd love to hear it.

Who we are

AI Safety Regensburg was started by Bastiaan Cnossen, a postdoctoral researcher in mathematics at the University of Regensburg.

We're looking for co-organisers: students, researchers or professionals, from any field. If you'd like to help shape what this group becomes, please get in touch.

We are an independent group, not affiliated with the University of Regensburg, and supported by Safe AI Germany, the national network of local AI safety groups.
